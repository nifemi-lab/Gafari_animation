How a video gets made

Six stages, in this order. You see and approve the work at each one before we move on.

  1. 1Script

    We tighten your idea into beats and jokes, timed to the second.

  2. 2Storyboard

    Rough sketches of every shot, so you see the story before we animate it.

  3. 3Animatic

    The storyboard cut together with a scratch voice track to lock the timing.

  4. 4Animation

    Characters, movement and effects drawn frame by frame.

  5. 5Sound

    Sound effects, music and voice mixed to the picture.

  6. 6Delivery

    Final files for YouTube, social and your archive, plus a round of fixes.
